How to Play

  1. A new Italian Brainrot character is featured every day.
  2. Read Clue 1 and type your guess in the text box.
  3. A correct guess earns you points — more points for fewer clues used.
  4. A wrong guess reveals the next clue, but reduces the maximum points.
  5. You have 5 clues in total. If you haven't guessed by then, the answer is revealed.
  6. Come back every day for a new character and keep your streak alive! 🔥
